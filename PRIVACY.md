# Privacy Policy

**GSM Auto-Answer** is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What the app stores

- App settings, including auto-answer preferences, SIM selection and appearance.
- Audio files you choose for the caller message and hold music, decoded into the app's
  private storage.
- Call-session events and a capability report used by the Diagnostics screen.

## What the app does not do

- It has no `INTERNET` permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or accounts.
- It does not record calls, upload audio or share data with third parties.
- It does not collect names, contacts, phone numbers or location.

## Permissions

Phone, call and notification permissions are used only to answer calls, show the in-call
screen, play caller audio and display call alerts. The protected permissions
`MODIFY_PHONE_STATE` and `MODIFY_AUDIO_ROUTING` are used only when the device firmware
grants them; a normal install cannot obtain them.

If you grant Shizuku access, the app uses it to read vendor audio configuration for the
device check and, when you explicitly confirm, to apply phone role, runtime permission,
battery and full-screen alert settings. Shizuku access can be revoked at any time in the
Shizuku app.

## Sharing and diagnostics

The Diagnostics and Device check screens can copy or share a report. Nothing is shared
automatically; a report leaves the device only when you choose Copy or Share.

## Data retention

All data stays in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes its settings,
audio cache and logs. You can also clear app storage from system settings.
